## 3. Legal Requirements Summary (NotebookLM Analysis)

### AMD Requirements (Cap. 577)
1. **Mental capacity** — maker must be capable at time of signing
2. **Voluntariness** — free will, no external pressure
3. **Writing** — AMD must be in writing
4. **Two witnesses** — one must be HK registered medical practitioner (RMP), second must be adult
5. **No conflict of interest** — witnesses must not benefit from maker's estate or insurance
6. **Informed decision** — must follow in-depth discussion with family + healthcare professionals
7. **Revocation** — "cautious making, easy revoking" principle

### Three Qualifying Health Conditions
1. **Terminal illness** (末期疾病)
2. **Persistent vegetative state / irreversible coma** (持續植物人狀況/不可逆轉昏迷)
3. **Other advanced irreversible life-limiting conditions** (其他晚期不可逆轉生存受限疾病)

## 4. AMD vs DNACPR vs EPA (NotebookLM Analysis)

| Dimension | AMD | DNACPR | EPA (持久授權書) |
|-----------|-----|--------|-------------------|
| **Purpose** | Refuse specific treatments | Refuse CPR specifically | Appoint attorney for affairs |
| **Who makes?** | Individual (maker) while capable | Doctor (RMP), after consensus | Individual (donor) while capable |
| **Scope** | Refuse LST (ventilation, dialysis, etc.) | DNACPR only | Delegate authority to third party |
| **When active** | When maker loses capacity + meets health criteria | During cardiac arrest | When donor becomes incapacitated |
| **Legal basis** | Cap. 577 Ordinance | Clinical order + Cap. 577 | Separate statutes |
